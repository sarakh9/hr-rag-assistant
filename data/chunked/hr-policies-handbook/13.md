## 8. Official Communication SLA Rules
* **Definitions:**
* *Service Level Agreement (SLA):* The maximum acceptable timeframe under which professional responses or acknowledgements must occur.
* *Primary Collaboration Platform:* Corporate approved digital tools optimized for messaging, document transmission, and teleconferencing.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Binds all personnel during designated active duty windows.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** All staff are responsible for maintaining standard communication availability metrics.
* **Approval Workflows:** Any permanent migration to alternative messaging applications for client accounts requires explicit operational vetting by the Director of IT.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* All official, standard corporate communications and file sharing must occur exclusively within approved platforms, primarily MS Teams or Slack, alongside Google Workspace or Zoom. Alternate paths (such as personal text, phone, or WhatsApp) are limited to urgent emergencies.
* **Response Service Level Agreements (SLAs):**
* *MS Teams / Chat Messages:* Responses must be sent within 10 minutes during active business hours.
* *Email Acknowledgment:* Receipt of external or internal emails must be acknowledged within 1 hour.
* *Email Final Resolution:* Full definitive resolution or structured updates must be delivered within 1 business day.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: Within what timeframe must I answer an internal MS Teams message?* A: Within 10 minutes during standard active operational hours.
* *Q: What is the email acknowledgment SLA requirement?* A: Inbound internal or external emails must be acknowledged within 1 hour.
