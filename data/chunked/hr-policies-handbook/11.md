## 7. Attendance, Leave Reporting & Desk Availability Guidelines
* **Definitions:**
* *Scrum Alignment:* The daily morning coordination meeting tracking development dependencies and task blockages.
* *Office Availability Channel:* The dedicated real-time chat pipeline used to publish temporary workflow absences.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Applies uniformly to all staff members during active business days.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Individual employees must broadcast their operational statuses. Project Managers review presence during daily alignment scrums.
* **Approval Workflows:** Planned leaves require a minimum of 1 week advance registration via the HR Portal for manager approval.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* For all scheduled time off, employees must notify the designated "Leave Plans" communication channel at least one week in advance, while simultaneously completing formal entry in the corporate HR portal to secure approval and ensure workload distribution.
* In case of sudden medical illness or unexpected personal emergencies, employees must update the "Leave Plans" channel between 9:30 AM and 10:00 AM, or prior to their scheduled morning scrum meeting (whichever occurs earlier). Direct notification to the manager via text or phone call must be completed as soon as possible.
* Presence at the daily morning scrum alignment is required for all assigned team members. Prior intimation to the Project Manager is expected if an emergency prevents attendance.
* Leave durations stretching to two or more consecutive business days require setting an official automated Out-of-Office (OOO) message on corporate mail and chat platforms to guide external and internal stakeholders.
* Temporary desk absences during the workday must be recorded transparently:
* *Short Breaks (1-2 Hours / Late Starts):* Employees must update the dedicated "Office Availability" channel with estimated return times.
* *MS Teams Status Formatting:*
* **Available:** Active working state.
