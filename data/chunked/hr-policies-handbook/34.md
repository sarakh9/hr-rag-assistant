## 20. Compensation Review & Salary Architecture
* **Definitions:**
* *Merit Increase:* A salary enhancement linked to high scores during performance evaluation cycles.
* *Market Adjustment:* A structural salary modification implemented to realign compensation with changing regional economic benchmarks.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Governs all active contract employees, with variations linked to tenure and role parameters.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Finance manages budget caps; Human Resources maps market compensation data benchmarks; the CEO role retains ultimate fiscal authorization.
* **Approval Workflows:** HR compensation analysis -> Budget matching by Finance -> Executive approval and signatures from the CEO role.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* Compensation bands and salary scales are designed based on objective industry market benchmarks, job weights, and regional cost-of-living differences. Payroll processing follows a structured monthly frequency at the end of each calendar month via direct deposit.
* Formal salary reviews occur on an annual cycle, correlating with year-end performance ratings. Salary changes are segmented into:
1. **Merit Increases:** Driven by performance evaluation scores.
2. **Market Adjustments:** Triggered by shifting regional economic benchmarks or recruitment talent scarcity data.
3. **Promotional Increases:** Calibrated whenever an employee shifts up a formal job tier.
* Employees receive a detailed monthly digital pay stub tracking base earnings, specific allowances, compliance withholdings, and Social Security Organization (SSO) metrics.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: How often do regular salary reviews occur?* A: Annually, aligning with the year-end performance assessment results.
* *Q: How frequently is payroll paid out?* A: Monthly frequency at the end of each calendar month.
