## 11. Prevention of Harassment & Misconduct Policy
* **Definitions:**
* *Harassment:* Unwelcome visual, verbal, or physical advances, gestures, or remarks that degrade individual dignity or construct a hostile work environment.
* *External Consultant:* An independent professional appointed to eliminate corporate bias during internal inquiries.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Protects all individuals across all physical offices, corporate vehicles, offsite events, and digital communication networks.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Enforcement and complaint resolution are handled by the Presiding Complaints Committee (PCC). The committee includes an internal staff representative (Software Engineer role) alongside a mandatory external member (Legal Advocate and Consultant role) to secure legal compliance and unbiased process execution.
* **Approval Workflows:** Complaints are logged via `compliance@company.com` or delivered directly to any PCC member.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* The organization maintains a zero-tolerance stance regarding sexual harassment or workplace misconduct, enforcing safety, mutual respect, and gender inclusivity across all corporate actions.
* Any individual experiencing workplace harassment can submit a formal written complaint within a maximum window of 3 months from the date of the incident. Detailed anonymous submissions are evaluated if they contain sufficient evidence to support fact-finding.
* **Standard Inquiry Workflow:**
1. *Acknowledgment:* The PCC issues a formal confirmation of receipt to the complainant within 3 working days.
2. *Impartial Investigation:* A formal inquiry is launched, guaranteeing both the complainant and the respondent a fair opportunity to present evidence, identify witnesses, and be heard.
3. *Completion Window:* The detailed inquiry is targeted to conclude within 90 days from the initial file receipt date.
