## 28. Workplace Health & Safety
* **Definitions:**
* *Incident Reporting:* The systematic logging of physical injuries, workplace accidents, or safety hazards within company borders.
* *Ergonomic Adjustment:* Adjusting workplace hardware or furniture setups to eliminate chronic strain and protect employee physical health.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Applies to all workers, visitors, contractors, and interns within any physical corporate facility or event operated by the organization.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Facilities management keeps safety assets operational; Safety Wardens lead safety drills; all individuals report workspace hazards.
* **Approval Workflows:** Hazard identification -> Facilities ticket logging -> Remedial safety verification signed off by the Operations Director within 48 hours.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* The organization is committed to providing a safe, secure, and healthy working environment. All physical facilities feature accessible first-aid kits, clearly mapped emergency evacuation exits, and fully functional fire suppression gear.
* Employees must report any physical injury, workspace accident, near-miss incident, or hazardous condition to their direct manager and the HR facilities team as soon as possible, or within a maximum window of 2 hours from the occurrence.
* Mandatory safety training, including building emergency evacuation procedures and fire drills, is organized bi-annually. Full employee participation is expected.
* The company provides ergonomic guidance and equipment adjustments to minimize workplace strain and promote physical well-being.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: What is the deadline to report a physical workplace injury or safety hazard?* A: It must be reported as soon as possible, or within a maximum window of 2 hours.
* *Q: How often are corporate safety drills and evacuation training sessions run?* A: Bi-annually, and participation is expected for all staff.
