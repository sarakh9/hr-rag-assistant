## 18. Promotion & Career Growth Policy
* **Definitions:**
* *Vertical Promotion:* Elevating an employee to a higher tier of professional title, ownership scope, and salary band.
* *Internal Mobility:* Progression allowing an employee to transition between cross-functional departments.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Full-time permanent personnel who have achieved a minimum of 12 consecutive months of service in their current active role with satisfactory performance scores.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Line Managers nominate candidates for elevation. Human Resources audits historical performance data. Executive leadership authorizes structural shifts.
* **Approval Workflows:** Manager nomination -> HR salary calibration review -> Final validation and compensation change approval signed by the CEO role.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* Promotion metrics are founded strictly on merit, demonstrated technical competence, and consistently exceeding established KPI targets over consecutive tracking periods.
* Open mid-level and senior positions are posted internally via the corporate bulletin or intranet for a minimum window of 5 business days before seeking external talent sourcing pools, maximizing internal mobility.
* Promotion review windows are synchronized with the annual performance evaluation cycles. Employees tracking a successful promotion receive an updated employment Addendum specifying their new corporate title, expanded accountability vectors, and corresponding merit-based salary modification.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: Can I apply for an internal department transfer if I have been with the company for 6 months?* A: No. A minimum of 12 months of consecutive service in your current role is required for internal mobility or promotion consideration.
* *Q: Are internal roles advertised to current employees?* A: Yes, they are broadcast on the intranet for at least 5 business days before external sourcing.
