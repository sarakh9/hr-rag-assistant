## 13. Data Privacy & Information Security  
### Q: Who can access my personal employee data, and how is it protected from AI systems?
A: Access to employee Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is limited strictly to authorized HR specialists, legal compliance staff, and verified payroll administrators on a need-to-know basis. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) models, internal AI assistants, and automated search indexes are completely isolated from tables containing restricted employee PII or salary structures to guarantee absolute privacy.
