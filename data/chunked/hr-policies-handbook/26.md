## 15. Employment Categories & Contracts Policy
* **Definitions:**
* *Permanent Worker:* Full-time or part-time staff signed to contracts with no predetermined end date, subject to statutory separation laws.
* *Temporary Worker:* Staff hired under contracts specifying an explicit calendar end date or project-based boundary.
* **Legal Requirements (Iran Labor Code):**
* Onboarding probationary periods must be explicitly stated in the written employment contract.
* Probationary limits are strictly capped by statutory law: a maximum of **1 month for unskilled and semi-skilled workers**, and a maximum of **3 months for skilled and professional employees**.
* During probation, either party may terminate the relationship immediately. Under statutory guidelines, if the employer terminates the relationship during probation, they may be liable for proportional wages for the active period but are exempt from standard severance pay liabilities.
* **Company Procedures & Contract Types:**
* All individuals executing labor for the organization must have an active, written contract detailing their operational titles, base salary structure, working hours distribution, and classification terms.
* Classifications are restricted to four core tracks:
1. **Permanent Full-Time:** Regular roles executing the 44-hour maximum workweek on indefinite tracks.
2. **Temporary/Fixed-Term:** Project-based roles limited by specific calendar milestones or operational cycles.
3. **Part-Time:** Positions executing less than the statutory weekly hours, compensated proportionally.
4. **Interns:** Educational training participants bound by explicit, short-term programmatic boundaries.
* Contract amendments, role revisions, or salary adjustments require a formal written Addendum signed by both the employee and an authorized HR representative. Agreements are stored securely in centralized digital repositories with highly restricted access profiles.
