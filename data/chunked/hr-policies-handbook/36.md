## 22. Grievance Resolution Policy
* **Definitions:**
* *Formal Grievance:* An official written report detailing a workplace concern, manager conflict, or perceived unfair treatment.
* *Mediation Conference:* A structured, neutral resolution meeting led by Human Resources to reconcile workplace disputes.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Open to every employee, intern, or contractor experiencing a workplace issue separate from Harassment or Whistleblower definitions.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Senior HR Executives manage case files and lead mediation. Department Heads assist with operational adjustments.
* **Approval Workflows:** Written grievance filing -> Preliminary HR review within 5 business days -> Mediation execution -> Binding resolution signed off by HR within 15 business days.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* The organization is committed to providing a professional work environment. Employees are encouraged to resolve minor operational or interpersonal issues informally with their direct supervisor first.
* If informal routes are ineffective or inappropriate, the employee may submit a Formal Grievance in writing directly to `hr@company.com`.
* The HR department will launch a review within 5 business days of submission, gathering relevant data and conducting separate discussions with involved parties.
* If necessary, a formal Mediation Conference will be organized to establish an actionable resolution. A final resolution report must be completed and logged within 15 business days of the initial filing. Retaliation against any individual for submitting a grievance in good faith is strictly prohibited.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: What is the email address to file a formal grievance?* A: Submit the written concern to `hr@company.com`.
* *Q: Within how many days must a formal grievance be resolved?* A: Within 15 business days of the initial filing.
