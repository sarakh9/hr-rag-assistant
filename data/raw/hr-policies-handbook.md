# HR Policies Handbook

## 1. Document Management & Review Policy
* **Definitions:**
    * *Policy Review Cycle:* The predetermined chronological schedule governing the evaluation of official company directives.
    * *Review Triggers:* Specific external or internal occurrences that mandate immediate, unscheduled policy adjustments.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** This policy applies globally to all operational guidelines, handbooks, and regulatory compliance documents managed by the organization.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Policy management is overseen by Compliance Officers, Human Resources, Legal Counsel, or designated Policy Owners. Research, writing, and drafting are handled by the Senior HR Executive role, followed by vetting and formal validation from the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) role.
* **Approval Workflows:** Policy modification draft -> Vetted by Legal Compliance -> Authorized by Executive Leadership -> Communicated to the workforce.
* **Operational Procedures:**
    * All organizational policies are reviewed annually or on an as-needed basis to maintain continuous alignment with regulatory requirements, industry best practices, and changing business objectives.
    * Immediate, non-scheduled reviews can be triggered by new legislation, shifts in core business priorities, corporate restructuring, technological advancements, or compliance gaps identified during incident reviews.
    * Updated policies feature a defined effective date, at which point they officially invalidate and supersede all prior iterations. Discrepancies or legacy disputes occurring prior to the effective date remain governed by the historical policy version in place at that time.
    * Official policies utilize standard closing markers such as "End of Policy Document" or "End of Document" to ensure structural transparency and eliminate confusion regarding missing materials or pages. This formatting does not alter the underlying validity of the text. Whenever a policy is amended or introduces new clauses, closing markers must be shifted to the new terminal edge of the text, and historical versions are archived for reference and audit trails.
    * Employees are informed of significant policy updates in advance through designated channels like company email broadcasts, internal memos, team meetings, or intranet announcements. Minor administrative edits are integrated into the master document without individual notifications.
    * Feedback on policy additions is encouraged. Employee suggestions are collected by Human Resources to refine operational relevance, encourage continuous improvement, and support policy optimization.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
    * *Q: Who drafts and who approves the HR policies?* A: The Senior HR Executive role drafts the policies, and the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) role formally validates and approves them.
    * *Q: How often are policies updated?* A: Formally on an annual cycle, or dynamically whenever legal, legislative, or operational triggers occur.

Reporting suspected or verified violations to the Compliance Team or via the reporting channel is mandatory for all personnel. Timely disclosure helps mitigate operational risks and protects organizational reputation.
    * All business transactions must be recorded promptly, completely, and accurately in accordance with established accounting controls. Falsification of documents, manipulation of financial sheets, or entering unverified expense structures is subject to disciplinary and legal reviews.
    * All internal staff members must complete regular anti-bribery and anti-corruption training sessions to ensure continuous alignment with regulatory compliance and ethics definitions.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**

* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Available to employees whose roles are authorized for remote fulfillment by department heads, provided they maintain consistent performance metrics.

## 6. Working Hours & Workweek Structure
* **Definitions:**
    * *Standard Workweek:* The maximum regular hours an individual can work at basic salary rates before triggering overtime premiums.
    * *Shift Work Allocation:* A rotating operational schedule across multi-hour blocks that balances project workloads.
* **Legal Requirements (Iran Labor Code):**
    * **Article 51:** Dictates that the regular workweek is established at a maximum of 44 hours.
    * **Article 52:** For difficult, injurious, underground, or hazardous job functions, working hours are strictly capped at 6 hours per day and 36 hours per week. Overtime is legally prohibited in these specific roles.
    * **Article 62:** Establishes Friday as the mandatory weekly rest day with full pay.
    * **Article 57:** Shift work cycles must not exceed a total of 176 hours across any consecutive 4-week period.
* **Company Procedures & Scheduling:**
    * The company's standard business schedule runs from Saturday to Wednesday (8 hours per day) and Thursday for a half-day (4 hours) to meet the 44-hour statutory framework.
    * Workers are provided a 30-minute rest and meal break after 6 consecutive hours of work. These breaks are non-working and unpaid unless otherwise specified by collective agreements or specific employment contracts.
    * Work performed between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM is classified as night work and is compensated at a 35% premium over standard hourly wages for non-shift workers.
    * Shift workers receive a monthly shift bonus of 10%, 15%, or 22.5% depending on the specific morning/evening/night shift rotation configured by the department head.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Human Resources ensures payroll configurations match statutory caps; Line Managers are responsible for structuring shift timetables within legal boundaries.
* **Approval Workflows:** Alterations to standard department shift models require explicit filing with Human Resources and final authorization by the Operations Director.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
    * *Q: What is the regular weekly hours maximum for standard roles?* A: 44 hours maximum per week, scheduled Saturday through Thursday.
    * *Q: What are the working hour limitations for hazardous positions?* A: Strictly 6 hours per day and 36 hours per week.

## 9. Overtime & Friday Compensation Policy
* **Definitions:**
    * *Overtime Premium Rate:* The legally mandated fiscal multiplier applied to normal base pay for hours worked beyond standard limits.
    * *Friday Compensatory Day Off:* A paid day of rest given inside the same week to substitute for working on the regular Friday holy day.
* **Legal Requirements (Iran Labor Code):**
    * **Article 59:** Overtime is legally permissible only under ordinary circumstances if the employee explicitly consents. Overtime must not exceed 4 hours per day except under extraordinary emergency situations.
    * Overtime compensation is legally fixed at a rate of 140% of the standard regular hourly wage (a 40% statutory premium).
    * Friday work legally mandates a 40% premium over the standard wage for all hours worked on that day, AND the employee must be granted a compulsory compensatory paid day off during the same week.
    * **Article 63:** Establishes full pay on all official state public holidays as well as Labour Day (May 1st), which is a mandatory paid holiday for all workers. Any work performed on these holidays is calculated using the overtime framework.
* **Company Procedures & Workflow:**
    * All extra hours must be authorized by managers in writing *before* the work is performed. Verbal approvals are not recognized for payroll tracking.
    * Human Resources tracks and reconciles all timesheet submissions via `hr@company.com`. Errors or unexpected deviations are routed back to the line manager for correction prior to the monthly payroll cutoff.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Human Resources Payroll calculates statutory overtime premiums. Managers audit and validate timesheets.
* **Approval Workflows:** Employees must receive prior explicit manager authorization before performing overtime hours.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
    * *Q: Can my manager require me to work overtime without my agreement?* A: No. Under Article 59 of the Iran Labour Code, overtime requires explicit employee consent.
    * *Q: What is the financial compensation for working on a public holiday or May 1st?* A: It is treated as overtime, paying 140% of the standard regular hourly rate.

## 11. Prevention of Harassment & Misconduct Policy
* **Definitions:**
    * *Harassment:* Unwelcome visual, verbal, or physical advances, gestures, or remarks that degrade individual dignity or construct a hostile work environment.
    * *External Consultant:* An independent professional appointed to eliminate corporate bias during internal inquiries.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Protects all individuals across all physical offices, corporate vehicles, offsite events, and digital communication networks.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Enforcement and complaint resolution are handled by the Presiding Complaints Committee (PCC). The committee includes an internal staff representative (Software Engineer role) alongside a mandatory external member (Legal Advocate and Consultant role) to secure legal compliance and unbiased process execution.
* **Approval Workflows:** Complaints are logged via `compliance@company.com` or delivered directly to any PCC member.
* **Operational Procedures:**
    * The organization maintains a zero-tolerance stance regarding sexual harassment or workplace misconduct, enforcing safety, mutual respect, and gender inclusivity across all corporate actions.
    * Any individual experiencing workplace harassment can submit a formal written complaint within a maximum window of 3 months from the date of the incident. Detailed anonymous submissions are evaluated if they contain sufficient evidence to support fact-finding.
    * **Standard Inquiry Workflow:**
        1. *Acknowledgment:* The PCC issues a formal confirmation of receipt to the complainant within 3 working days.
        2. *Impartial Investigation:* A formal inquiry is launched, guaranteeing both the complainant and the respondent a fair opportunity to present evidence, identify witnesses, and be heard.
        3. *Completion Window:* The detailed inquiry is targeted to conclude within 90 days from the initial file receipt date.
        4. *Management Escalation:* The final investigative report, including recommended corrective actions, is submitted to executive management within 10 days of inquiry closure.
    * Anti-retaliation protections apply fully to complainants, supporting witnesses, and PCC panel members. Victimization or harassment of individuals involved in an inquiry is treated as a severe offense.
    * All proceedings, interview recordings, witness identities, and case files are kept strictly confidential. Access is restricted exclusively to authorized panel members. 
    * If an inquiry reveals that a complaint was intentionally fabricated or brought forward with malicious intent in bad faith, management reserves the right to impose disciplinary penalties on the filer.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
    * *Q: Who serves on the complaints panel?* A: An internal staff representative (Software Engineer role) and an independent external Legal Consultant.
    * *Q: What is the maximum timeframe allowed to file a complaint after an incident?* A: A maximum window of 3 months.

## 15. Employment Categories & Contracts Policy
* **Definitions:**
    * *Permanent Worker:* Full-time or part-time staff signed to contracts with no predetermined end date, subject to statutory separation laws.
    * *Temporary Worker:* Staff hired under contracts specifying an explicit calendar end date or project-based boundary.
* **Legal Requirements (Iran Labor Code):**
    * Onboarding probationary periods must be explicitly stated in the written employment contract.
    * Probationary limits are strictly capped by statutory law: a maximum of **1 month for unskilled and semi-skilled workers**, and a maximum of **3 months for skilled and professional employees**.
    * During probation, either party may terminate the relationship immediately. Under statutory guidelines, if the employer terminates the relationship during probation, they may be liable for proportional wages for the active period but are exempt from standard severance pay liabilities.
* **Company Procedures & Contract Types:**
    * All individuals executing labor for the organization must have an active, written contract detailing their operational titles, base salary structure, working hours distribution, and classification terms.
    * Classifications are restricted to four core tracks:
        1. **Permanent Full-Time:** Regular roles executing the 44-hour maximum workweek on indefinite tracks.
        2. **Temporary/Fixed-Term:** Project-based roles limited by specific calendar milestones or operational cycles.
        3. **Part-Time:** Positions executing less than the statutory weekly hours, compensated proportionally.
        4. **Interns:** Educational training participants bound by explicit, short-term programmatic boundaries.
    * Contract amendments, role revisions, or salary adjustments require a formal written Addendum signed by both the employee and an authorized HR representative. Agreements are stored securely in centralized digital repositories with highly restricted access profiles.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Human Resources compliance updates the master contract templates. Legal Counsel audits contract adjustments. Employees sign acknowledgements.
* **Approval Workflows:** Standard template deployment -> Manager specific additions -> Final operational and financial budget authorization from the CEO role.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
    * *Q: What is the maximum permitted probation period for a professional engineer?* A: A maximum of 3 months under standard labor provisions.
    * *Q: Can an employment contract be amended verbally?* A: No. All modifications require a formal written Addendum signed by both parties.
