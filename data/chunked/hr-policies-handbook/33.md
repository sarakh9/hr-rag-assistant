* *Legal Review of Training Repayment Clauses:* To comply with applicable labor laws, the company does not enforce automatic financial penalties or mandatory salary deductions for training costs if an employee resigns. Any training cost repayment obligations must be governed by a separate, specialized, mutually signed **Educational Bond Agreement** executed *prior* to the training. This bond must use a legally validated, non-punitive, sliding prorated scale restricted strictly to direct out-of-pocket tuition costs, and cannot be deducted unilaterally from statutory final settlements without formal review.
