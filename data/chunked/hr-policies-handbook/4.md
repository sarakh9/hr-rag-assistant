## 3. Anti-Bribery, Anti-Corruption, & Financial Integrity
* **Definitions:**
* *Bribe:* Giving, offering, receiving, or soliciting anything of value to improperly influence an official or commercial decision.
* *Kickback:* An illegal payment made back to a facilitator as a reward for securing a business transaction.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Applicable to all full-time, part-time, probationary, and contract personnel, alongside third-party agents representing the organization.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Individual employees must maintain compliant behavior. The Compliance Team handles the implementation, regular monitoring, and enforcement of anti-corruption guidelines.
* **Approval Workflows:** Any suspicious transaction or request for compliance vetting must be logged immediately with the Compliance Team.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* The organization maintains a zero-tolerance stance against offering, promising, giving, soliciting, or accepting any bribe, kickback, corrupt payment, or unethical incentive, whether directly or indirectly, to influence a business choice or gain an improper advantage.
* The Compliance Team operates as the designated point of contact for scenario-specific advice and interpretation of acceptable practices. Reporting suspected or verified violations to the Compliance Team or via the reporting channel is mandatory for all personnel. Timely disclosure helps mitigate operational risks and protects organizational reputation.
* All business transactions must be recorded promptly, completely, and accurately in accordance with established accounting controls. Falsification of documents, manipulation of financial sheets, or entering unverified expense structures is subject to disciplinary and legal reviews.
* All internal staff members must complete regular anti-bribery and anti-corruption training sessions to ensure continuous alignment with regulatory compliance and ethics definitions.
