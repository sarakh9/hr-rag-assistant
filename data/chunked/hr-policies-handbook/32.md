## 19. Learning & Development Policy
* **Definitions:**
* *Educational Sponsorship:* Corporate financial funding deployed to cover professional certifications or academic courses.
* *Knowledge-Sharing Workshop:* An internal instructional seminar where sponsored employees deliver educational reviews to their wider team units.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Open to permanent staff who have successfully completed their contract probation parameters.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Individual employees submit growth roadmaps. Department Heads allocate project budgets. Human Resources updates the master learning management tracking tools.
* **Approval Workflows:** Training Request Form submission -> Cost authorization from the Department Head -> HR registration and voucher processing.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* The organization promotes an environment of continuous learning, offering annual learning allocations or educational sponsorships to eligible employees for approved technical certifications, advanced industry courses, and professional skill seminars.
* Educational programs must hold direct, clear relevance to the employee's active functional role or immediate internal career progression roadmap.
* Upon completion of a corporate-funded course or certification path, the recipient is expected to organize and host an internal Knowledge-Sharing Workshop within 30 days to distribute the technical insights to their respective department peers.
