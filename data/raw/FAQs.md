# HR Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) 

## 1. Document Management & Policy Reviews
### Q: How often is the company policy reviewed and updated?
A: All organizational policies are reviewed on a formal annual cycle or dynamically as needed to maintain alignment with regulatory shifts, legal updates, and evolving industry best practices.

### Q: Will employees be notified of major adjustments to policies?
A: Yes. Significant policy changes are communicated in advance through official company channels such as corporate email broadcasts, internal memos, or intranet announcements. Minor administrative edits are integrated seamlessly into the master text without individual alerts.

### Q: What is the significance of the "End of Document" text or markers at the conclusion of policies?
A: These standard closing markers are used strictly for document formatting and structural transparency. They confirm that the material is complete and that no pages or sections are missing; they have no effect on the underlying legal validity of the text.

### Q: Can employees provide input on corporate policies?
A: Yes. Feedback on policy adjustments is actively encouraged. Employee suggestions are collected by Human Resources to continuously improve operational relevance and optimize workplace practices.

## 2. Code of Business Conduct, Ethics & Compliance
### Q: What core values guide corporate operations?
A: The organization is committed to professional growth founded upon transparency, responsibility, corporate integrity, compliance, and an inclusive work environment.

### Q: Is it mandatory to sign or acknowledge the Code of Conduct?
A: Yes. Every employee, contractor, and relevant stakeholder is expected to read, understand, and provide a written or electronic self-declaration acknowledging their commitment to the Code of Conduct during onboarding and following any major updates.

### Q: Where should I direct questions regarding company procedures or ethical concerns?
A: General handbook or policy questions should be directed to Human Resources at `hr@company.com`. Complex ethical or compliance questions can be escalated to the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) at `ceo@company.com` or the Policy Compliance Committee (PCC).

### Q: Are third-party vendors and contractors bound by the same ethical guidelines?
A: Yes. External vendors and contractors are expected to fulfill the operational, compliance, and ethical principles dictated by the organization. Failure to maintain these standards will result in a formal review and potential termination of the vendor contract.

## 3. Anti-Bribery, Anti-Corruption & Financial Integrity
### Q: What is the organization's stance on bribes or kickbacks?
A: The organization maintains a zero-tolerance stance against offering, promising, giving, soliciting, or accepting any bribe, kickback, corrupt payment, or unethical incentive, whether directly or indirectly.

### Q: Who handles the monitoring and enforcement of anti-corruption guidelines?
A: The corporate Compliance Team handles the implementation, regular monitoring, and enforcement of anti-corruption guidelines, serving as the designated point of contact for scenario-specific advice.

### Q: Is it acceptable to modify or alter expense records if a client requests it?
A: No. All business transactions must be recorded promptly, completely, and accurately. Falsification of documents or manipulating financial structures is strictly prohibited and triggers a disciplinary and legal review.

### Q: Are employees required to undergo anti-corruption training?
A: Yes. All internal staff members must complete regular anti-bribery and anti-corruption training sessions to ensure continuous alignment with regulatory compliance and ethics definitions.

## 4. Gifts, Entertainment & Business Hospitality
### Q: Do I need to disclose business gifts or hospitality offers?
A: Yes. All business gifts, entertainment, or hospitality offers exceeding the company’s specified value threshold must be reported and formally documented.

### Q: What should I do if local cultural customs make it impractical to decline a business gift?
A: If refusing a token gift fundamentally compromises professional etiquette or regional corporate relations, you should accept the item politely on behalf of the company and promptly report it to Human Resources for guidance.

### Q: How does management allocate gifts accepted on behalf of the company?
A: Management evaluates the item and determines the appropriate channel, which may include donating it to charity, sharing it collectively with the team, or registering it as a permanent company asset.

## 5. Work From Home (WFH) & Remote Workspace Security
### Q: What general standards apply to remote work and working from home?
A: Remote work balances flexibility, trust, and accountability. Remote workers are expected to maintain professional output, clear communication, and consistent alignment with company standards.

### Q: What are the reachability requirements for remote employees?
A: Remote employees must remain reachable via approved corporate communication channels (phone, email, MS Teams, or Slack) throughout active working hours. Attendance at scheduled project, department, and team alignment meetings is required.

### Q: Can I use personal hardware or let family members access my work device?
A: No. All official business must be conducted exclusively on company-provided hardware. Personal devices are restricted from accessing sensitive networks. To maintain complete device isolation, family members or third parties are restricted from accessing company systems.

### Q: What happens if an employee fails to follow remote work expectations?
A: Continued deviations from WFH expectations trigger a progressive performance management sequence, focusing on remediation:
1. *First Occurrence:* Verbal or written discussion and performance tracking.
2. *Second Occurrence:* Temporary suspension of WFH privileges and a transition to mandatory office check-ins.
3. *Third Occurrence:* Review of remote privileges, which may require a return to the physical office space, or further evaluation under the disciplinary framework.

## 6. Working Hours, Breaks & Shift Work Structures
### Q: What is the maximum regular workweek structure under local statutory law?
A: In absolute compliance with Article 51 of the Iran Labor Code, the regular workweek is established at a maximum of 44 hours. The standard business schedule runs from Saturday to Wednesday (8 hours per day) and Thursday for a half-day (4 hours).

### Q: Are daily rest and meal breaks included as active working hours?
A: Workers are provided a 30-minute rest and meal break after 6 consecutive hours of work. These breaks are non-working and unpaid unless otherwise specified by collective agreements or explicit employment contracts.

### Q: What is the weekly rest day entitlement?
A: Pursuant to Article 62 of the Iran Labor Code, Friday is the mandatory weekly rest day with full pay.

### Q: What are the working hour limitations and rules for hazardous roles?
A: Pursuant to Article 52, for difficult, injurious, underground, or hazardous job functions, working hours are strictly capped at 6 hours per day and 36 hours per week. Overtime is legally prohibited in these specific roles.

### Q: How is night work or shift work compensated?
A: Work performed between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM is classified as night work and is compensated at a 35% premium for non-shift workers. Shift workers receive a monthly shift bonus of 10%, 15%, or 22.5% depending on the rotation cycle configured by their department head, provided hours do not exceed 176 hours across any consecutive 4-week period (Article 57).

## 7. Attendance, Unplanned Absences & Desktop Availability
### Q: What is the procedure for reporting an unplanned or emergency leave?
A: In case of sudden medical illness or unexpected personal emergencies, employees must update the dedicated "Leave Plans" channel between 9:30 AM and 10:00 AM, or prior to their scheduled morning scrum meeting (whichever occurs earlier). Direct notification to the manager via text or phone call must also be completed as soon as possible.

### Q: Is attendance mandatory for the daily morning scrum meeting?
A: Yes. Presence at the daily morning scrum alignment is required for all assigned team members. Prior intimation to the Project Manager is expected if an emergency prevents attendance.

### Q: How should I update my desk availability during short absences or lunch?
A: Temporary desk absences must be recorded transparently:
* *Short Breaks (1-2 Hours):* Update the dedicated "Office Availability" channel with estimated return times.
* *MS Teams Status Formatting:* Use **Available** for active work, **Be Right Back (BRB)** for temporary 10–15 minute breaks, **Away** (with a status message) for scheduled lunch breaks, and **Offline** (with a status message) for end-of-day logout or active approved leave.

## 8. Communication Responsibilities & Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
### Q: What are the primary communication platforms authorized by the company?
A: All official corporate communications and file sharing must occur exclusively within approved platforms, primarily MS Teams or Slack, alongside Google Workspace or Zoom. Alternate paths (such as WhatsApp) are limited to urgent emergencies.

### Q: What are the mandatory response SLAs during active business hours?
A: 
* *MS Teams / Chat Messages:* Responses must be sent within 10 minutes.
* *Email Acknowledgment:* Inbound internal or external emails must be acknowledged within 1 hour.
* *Email Final Resolution:* Full definitive resolution or structured updates must be delivered within 1 business day.

## 9. Overtime Regulations & Public Holidays
### Q: What are the legal criteria for executing overtime hours?
A: Pursuant to Article 59 of the Iran Labor Code, working overtime is permissible only under ordinary circumstances if the employee explicitly consents. Overtime must not exceed 4 hours per day except under extraordinary emergencies, and is compensated at a rate of 140% of the standard hourly wage.

### Q: What happens if I am required to work on a Friday?
A: Friday work legally mandates a 40% premium over the standard wage for all hours worked on that day, AND the employee must be granted a compulsory compensatory paid day off during the same week.

### Q: Are public holidays paid, and how is work on those days calculated?
A: Yes. Employees are entitled to full pay on all official state public holidays as well as Labour Day (May 1st), under Article 63. Any authorized work performed on these holidays is calculated using the overtime framework (140% rate). All overtime must be authorized by managers in writing before the work is performed.

## 10. Whistleblower Protections & Reporting Channels
### Q: How can an employee report a serious compliance breach or financial fraud anonymously?
A: Disclosures can be routed confidentially to the designated Compliance Officer, submitted via email to `ethics@company.com`, or dropped into corporate physical suggestion boxes or secure online forms designed for anonymous entries.

### Q: What is the investigation timeline for a whistleblower report?
A: Non-anonymous reports are acknowledged within 3 to 5 business days. Initial reviews determine if a formal inquiry is warranted. Standard cases are targeted for full resolution within 30 days, with status updates provided where legally permissible.

### Q: What protections exist against retaliation for whistleblowers?
A: The company strictly prohibits any form of retaliation, harassment, or discrimination against an individual who reports an ethical concern or provides witness testimony in good faith. Verified acts of retaliation are treated as major policy violations subject to the corrective framework.

## 11. Prevention of Harassment & Grievance Resolution
### Q: How does the company handle harassment or workplace misconduct complaints?
A: The organization maintains a zero-tolerance stance regarding workplace harassment, monitored by the Presiding Complaints Committee (PCC). The panel features an internal staff representative (Software Engineer role) and an independent external Legal Consultant to secure objective compliance.

### Q: What is the timeline for filing and investigating a harassment complaint?
A: Formal written complaints must be submitted within a maximum window of 3 months from the date of the incident to `compliance@company.com`. Inquiries are acknowledged within 3 working days and are targeted to conclude within 90 days.

### Q: What is the difference between a grievance and a whistleblower report, and how are grievances handled?
A: Grievances cover general workplace concerns, manager conflicts, or team disputes rather than legal or financial fraud. Formal grievances should be sent in writing to `hr@company.com`. HR launches a review within 5 business days and hosts a Mediation Conference if necessary, targeting a resolution within 15 business days.

## 12. Performance Management, Career Progression & Learning
### Q: How are performance reviews and KPIs structured?
A: Performance cycles follow an annual structure, segmented into Q1 goal alignment, mid-year progression reviews, and year-end comprehensive objective assessments using balanced scorecards.

### Q: What happens if an employee's performance metrics are consistently deficient?
A: A formal written Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) lasting 30, 60, or a maximum of 90 calendar days will be initialized with clear, measurable targets. Regular alignment meetings occur to log progress.

### Q: What criteria govern promotions and internal department transfers?
A: Promotions are based strictly on merit, demonstrated competence, and consistently exceeding targets. Open positions are posted internally for a minimum of 5 business days. To apply for a transfer or promotion, a minimum of 12 months of consecutive service in your current role is required.

### Q: Can the company automatically deduct training costs from my salary if I resign?
A: No. Automatic salary deductions for training costs are restricted. Any training cost repayment obligations must be outlined in a separate, mutually signed Educational Bond Agreement executed prior to the training, using a legally validated, non-punitive, sliding prorated scale restricted to out-of-pocket tuition costs.

## 13. Data Privacy & Information Security
### Q: Who can access my personal employee data, and how is it protected from AI systems?
A: Access to employee Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is limited strictly to authorized HR specialists, legal compliance staff, and verified payroll administrators on a need-to-know basis. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) models, internal AI assistants, and automated search indexes are completely isolated from tables containing restricted employee PII or salary structures to guarantee absolute privacy.

### Q: What are the security rules for corporate passwords and accounts?
A: User profiles must utilize strong passwords (minimum 14 characters, combining alphanumeric and special characters) paired with active, mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA). Passwords must be updated every 90 days, and sharing accounts or authentication tokens is entirely banned. Security incidents must be reported to the IT Security Desk within 1 hour.

---

## 14. Offboarding, Equipment Returns & Severance Pay
### Q: What notice period is expected for a voluntary resignation?
A: Full-time permanent workers are expected to provide a formal written resignation notice at least 1 month in advance to ensure an adequate knowledge transfer period.

### Q: Can the company automatically deduct money from my final paycheck if my equipment is lost or damaged?
A: No. The company will not make automatic or unilateral deductions from an employee's salary for damaged or unreturned equipment unless explicit written consent is provided by the employee at the time of the event, or a formal legal determination is established. Unreturned items are pursued via standard equipment return protocols and legal reclamation paths.

### Q: How is severance pay (End of Service Benefit) calculated?
A: In strict accordance with the Iran Labor Code, upon separation of employment, redundancy, or retirement, all employees are legally entitled to an End of Service Benefit. The company pays a minimum of 1 full month’s last base salary for every year of continuous service completed, along with a full payout of any remaining valid, unused annual leave balance (up to the legal 9-day annual carryover cap plus current year accruals).
