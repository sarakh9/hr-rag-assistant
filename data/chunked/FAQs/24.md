## 7. Attendance, Unplanned Absences & Desktop Availability  
### Q: What is the procedure for reporting an unplanned or emergency leave?
A: In case of sudden medical illness or unexpected personal emergencies, employees must update the dedicated "Leave Plans" channel between 9:30 AM and 10:00 AM, or prior to their scheduled morning scrum meeting (whichever occurs earlier). Direct notification to the manager via text or phone call must also be completed as soon as possible.
