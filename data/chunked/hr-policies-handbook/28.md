## 16. Onboarding & Background Verification
* **Definitions:**
* *Onboarding Orientation:* The systematic introductory pathway tracking cultural integration and security setup for fresh hires.
* *Credential Verification:* Direct authentication of academic transcripts, diplomas, or professional references.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Mandated for 100% of newly contracted personnel joining the company, without exception.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** IT Provisioning sets up hardware profiles; Human Resources coordinates systemic compliance orientations; Line Managers deliver domain-specific workflow objectives.
* **Approval Workflows:** Pre-arrival IT ticketing -> Document upload verification in the HR Portal -> Onboarding checkpoint confirmation signed by HR within the first 5 working days.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* The first day of employment must follow a structured orientation itinerary covering company culture, organizational structure, data security controls, and immediate benefits enrollment configurations.
* New hires must supply required documentation (including identification papers, tax documents, academic certificates, and background records) into the secure corporate portal during or prior to day one.
* IT Provisioning configures and distributes designated corporate hardware assets, active access keys, and secure encrypted user accounts on day one.
* Completion of compliance training tracks, including review and digital sign-off acknowledging the Code of Conduct and specific data privacy provisions, is required within the first week of active employment.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: When must a new hire sign off on the handbook and policy disclosures?* A: Within the first week of active onboarding.
* *Q: Who sets up the hardware for the new hire?* A: IT Provisioning delivers company-owned assets on the first day of employment.
