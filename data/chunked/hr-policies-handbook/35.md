## 21. Employee Benefits Policy
* **Definitions:**
* *Statutory Bonus:* Legally mandated annual fiscal distributions paid out to workers.
* *Allowance Tracking:* Monthly non-base cash supplements designed to offset transportation or subsistence costs.
* **Legal Requirements (Iran Labor Code):**
* All employers are required to pay employees who have completed one full year of service a mandatory New Year gift and annual bonus equivalent to **two months' of their base salary**.
* Core health benefits, medical care coverage, and retirement security profiles are organized through mandatory contributions to the Social Security Organization (SSO). The employer contributes the statutory 23%, and the employee contributes 7%.
* **Company Procedures:**
* Baseline enrollment into the social security benefit interface is completed automatically upon day one of employment onboarding.
* The company provides structured monthly cash supplements for qualified positions, including a fixed Transportation Allowance and a Meal Allowance, built directly into the monthly payroll interface.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Benefits Administration tracks health enrollments; Payroll structures the statutory gift payouts.
* **Approval Workflows:** Automatic baseline enrollment at hire -> Annual bonus calculation verification by Finance -> CEO role authorization.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: Is there a 13th-month salary bonus?* A: No, but there is a mandatory statutory New Year Gift equivalent to two months' base salary for employees with one full year of tenure.
* *Q: What are the contribution rates for Social Security (SSO)?* A: The employer contributes 23%, and the employee contributes 7%.
