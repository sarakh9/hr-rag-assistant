## 12. Corrective Guidance & Disciplinary Actions Framework
* **Definitions:**
* *Corrective Action:* A progressive structural approach designed to address gaps in performance, behavior, or policy compliance.
* *Information Security Boundary:* Operational limitations on transmitting proprietary source codes or sensitive organizational databases.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Uniformly binds all employment categories, consultants, and interns across the organization.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Line managers identify performance or policy infractions; Human Resources implements the appropriate level of the progressive framework; Senior Management signs off on structural termination parameters.
* **Approval Workflows:** Allegations are reviewed by the Zero Tolerance Review Panel via `hr@company.com` prior to enforcement of suspensions or separations.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* The organization enforces a structured corrective guidance framework against actions that compromise workplace safety, operational integrity, or mutual respect. Prohibited activities include discrimination, harassment, physical bullying, spreading malicious rumors, insubordination, reporting to work under the influence of substances, unauthorized data disclosure, and intentional timeline obstruction.
* The possession, consumption, sale, or distribution of illegal narcotics or unprescribed controlled substances on company premises or during active hours is entirely banned. Alcohol consumption during active hours or reporting to work under the influence is prohibited. Legally authorized prescription medications are allowed provided they are used safely according to medical directions.
* Confidential company data includes financial reports, forward business strategies, salary structures, personnel assessments, client lists, source code, software architecture, internal memos, and meeting minutes. Sharing this data with unauthorized internal staff or external parties without explicit management clearance is a major policy violation.
