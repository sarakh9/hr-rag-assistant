## 25. Social Media Policy
* **Definitions:**
* *Public Statement:* Any text, video, or digital post that can be explicitly linked back to the company or its corporate brand image.
* *Confidential Disclosure:* Releasing corporate source code, workspace environments, or internal client rosters online.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Applies to all staff members maintaining active personal or professional profiles on public social media channels.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Public Relations manages official corporate brand representation; individual staff are responsible for personal posts.
* **Approval Workflows:** Speaking on behalf of the company or publishing corporate marketing content requires written clearance from the PR Director.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* Employees are prohibited from publishing public statements or digital posts that claim to represent the official views of the organization without prior written clearance from the PR Director.
* Displaying confidential company data, proprietary software screens, internal strategy presentations, client rosters, or secure workplace environments in public photos or text updates is prohibited.
* Personal digital activity must not leverage company logos, brand assets, or trademarked material unless explicitly authorized for official marketing distributions.
* Postings containing discriminatory text, harassment, defaming statements, or illegal content that compromises corporate values or reputation will be evaluated under the corrective guidance framework.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: Can I post a photo of my development screen showing product source code on social media?* A: No. Displaying corporate source code or internal strategy layouts is a policy violation.
* *Q: Am I allowed to speak on behalf of the company at a public tech panel?* A: Only if you have received prior written clearance from the PR Director.
