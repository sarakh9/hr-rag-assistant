## 13. Corporate Asset Management & Offboarding Infrastructure
* **Definitions:**
* *Corporate Infrastructure:* Physical items (laptops, mobile phones) and digital licensing resources owned exclusively by the organization.
* *Replacement Value Review:* An objective assessment of outstanding property value, subject to legal limits regarding direct salary deductions.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Enforced upon all users granted ownership or access tokens to company hardware assets.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** IT Operations handles deployment and checking-in of equipment; Human Resources and Finance calculate appropriate final settlements following strict legal guidelines.
* **Approval Workflows:** Hardware issuance and post-termination return logs must be signed off by the IT Asset Manager before financial clearance releases.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* Company-provided infrastructure, including laptops, mobile phones, peripheral devices, software licenses, network bandwidth, and intellectual property, must be managed responsibly and used exclusively for legitimate business purposes. Utilizing corporate hardware or tools for personal freelance projects or external personal gain is restricted.
* Employees are expected to report any observed destruction, misuse, or theft of corporate assets to their supervisor or Human Resources.
* Upon separation of employment, contract completion, or at management’s direct request, the individual must return all company-owned hardware, security tokens, data media, and office tools in good working condition within the stipulated time frame defined by the exit guidelines.
* If an employee accidentally damages company equipment during their tenure or prior to offboarding, they must report it to IT or operations immediately. Management will evaluate the damage.
