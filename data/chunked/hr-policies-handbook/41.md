## 26. Internal Transfer Policy
* **Definitions:**
* *Cross-Functional Transfer:* Relocating an employee between entirely distinct business units or technical disciplines.
* *Operational Backfill:* Sourcing a qualified candidate to replace an employee who has transferred out of their department.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Open to permanent staff tracking a minimum of 12 months of active, satisfactory service within their current department role.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Sourcing managers evaluate candidates; releasing managers schedule transfer timelines; Human Resources facilitates contract adjustments.
* **Approval Workflows:** Employee application -> Vetting by current and receiving managers -> HR transfer addendum signed by the CEO role.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* Internal transfers allow employees to apply for open job roles in other departments, supporting professional development and internal mobility.
* The applicant must notify their current line manager before interviewing for an internal position.
* If the transfer is approved, the releasing manager and receiving manager collaborate to establish a transition schedule. This transition timeline cannot exceed 30 calendar days from the date of official transfer approval, ensuring operational continuity and enabling appropriate backfill tracking.
* Salary structures and benefit profiles will be calibrated by HR to match the compensation bands of the new job tier.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: Do I need to tell my manager before applying for an internal job vacancy?* A: Yes, you must notify your current line manager before participating in internal department interviews.
* *Q: What is the maximum transition period allowed to move between roles?* A: The formal transition schedule cannot exceed 30 calendar days from the date of approval.
