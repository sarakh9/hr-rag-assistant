## 5. Work From Home (WFH) & Remote Work Security
* **Definitions:**
* *Device Isolation:* Restricting corporate assets from sharing networks or configurations with unapproved personal devices or family members.
* *Escalation Sequence:* A standardized multi-tiered progressive guidance track applied upon deviation from remote operational parameters.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Available to employees whose roles are authorized for remote fulfillment by department heads, provided they maintain consistent performance metrics.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Employees must maintain reachability. Line managers track daily attendance and deliverable timelines.
* **Approval Workflows:** Remote privileges are initially approved by Department Heads and formal entry is tracked in the HR Portal.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* The corporate approach to remote work balances flexibility, trust, and accountability. Remote workers are expected to maintain professional output, clear communication, and consistent alignment with company standards.
* Remote employees must remain reachable via approved corporate communication channels (phone, email, MS Teams, or Slack) throughout their active working hours. Attendance at scheduled project, department, and team alignment meetings is required unless an explicit prior exception is granted by the reporting manager.
* Employees working from home must maintain a quiet, secure, and distraction-free workspace that supports productivity and ensures professional call and video environments.
* All official business must be conducted exclusively on company-provided hardware. Personal devices are restricted from accessing sensitive company data networks. Employees must comply with IT protection policies, utilize corporate encryption, maintain strict password confidentiality, and prevent unauthorized family members or third parties from accessing company systems.
