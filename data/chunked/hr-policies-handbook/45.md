## 30. Employee Exit & Separation Policy
* **Definitions:**
* *End of Service Benefit:* The legally mandated financial distribution paid out to separating workers based on cumulative tenure.
* *Exit Clearance Form:* A centralized compliance log tracking asset collection, financial balancing, and data access termination.
* **Legal Requirements (Iran Labor Code):**
* **Severance Pay Formulation:** Upon separation of employment, redundancy, or retirement, all employees are legally entitled to a mandatory End of Service Benefit. The company pays a minimum of **1 full month’s last base salary for every year of continuous service** completed. Pro-rata distributions apply for partial years.
* **Accrued Unused Leave Settlement:** Any remaining valid, unused annual leave balance (up to the legal 9-day annual carryover cap plus current year accruals) must be fully paid out as a financial distribution in the final financial settlement.
* **Termination Protections:** Involuntary separations outside the standard probation window must be based on clear, legally recognized statutory grounds. Arbitrary terminations are restricted under local regulations.
* **Company Procedures & Offboarding:**
* **Voluntary Resignation Notice:** Full-time permanent workers are expected to provide a formal written resignation notice at least 1 month in advance, in accordance with standard professional customs, to ensure an adequate knowledge transfer period.
* Separating employees are requested to dedicate their remaining notice weeks to organizing operational assets, running training handovers, and completing the corporate Knowledge Transfer Protocol before final clearance sign-off.
* Behavioral breaches under the corrective guidance framework are subject to review by the internal compliance board prior to any final contract termination actions.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Human Resources offboarding processes clearances; Finance calculates statutory severance pay; IT revokes access permissions.
