## 29. Business Continuity Policy
* **Definitions:**
* *Disaster Recovery Mode:* Activating emergency backup communication links and secondary data paths during critical system outages.
* *Alternative Operating Location:* Prescribed remote work frameworks activated when physical offices suffer localized disasters.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Binds all functional teams and systems infrastructure operations during emergency disruptions.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** The Business Continuity Team coordinates emergency directives; IT executes automated data failovers; employees maintain communication readiness.
* **Approval Workflows:** Emergency declaration by the CEO role -> Activation of Disaster Recovery channels by IT -> Return to Normal Operations sign-off by the Executive Board.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* In the event of infrastructure failure, power grid outages, regional disasters, or public health emergencies, the company will transition into Disaster Recovery Mode.
* The IT Infrastructure Team maintains secure, redundant cloud architecture and daily automated data backups to protect data assets and ensure system availability.
* Employees will be instructed via emergency broadcast updates (SMS, alternative email, or phone trees) to transition to remote operations under specific WFH safety profiles.
* Critical business units must remain responsive through alternative communication platforms designated by the Business Continuity Team within 2 hours of emergency activation.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: How often are company data backups performed?* A: Daily and automatically, secured via redundant cloud architecture.
* *Q: What should I do if a power outage shuts down the main physical office infrastructure?* A: Monitor alternative communication links for emergency broadcast updates and prepare to activate remote WFH backup protocols.
