## 6. Working Hours & Workweek Structure
* **Definitions:**
* *Standard Workweek:* The maximum regular hours an individual can work at basic salary rates before triggering overtime premiums.
* *Shift Work Allocation:* A rotating operational schedule across multi-hour blocks that balances project workloads.
* **Legal Requirements (Iran Labor Code):**
* **Article 51:** Dictates that the regular workweek is established at a maximum of 44 hours.
* **Article 52:** For difficult, injurious, underground, or hazardous job functions, working hours are strictly capped at 6 hours per day and 36 hours per week. Overtime is legally prohibited in these specific roles.
* **Article 62:** Establishes Friday as the mandatory weekly rest day with full pay.
* **Article 57:** Shift work cycles must not exceed a total of 176 hours across any consecutive 4-week period.
* **Company Procedures & Scheduling:**
* The company's standard business schedule runs from Saturday to Wednesday (8 hours per day) and Thursday for a half-day (4 hours) to meet the 44-hour statutory framework.
* Workers are provided a 30-minute rest and meal break after 6 consecutive hours of work. These breaks are non-working and unpaid unless otherwise specified by collective agreements or specific employment contracts.
* Work performed between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM is classified as night work and is compensated at a 35% premium over standard hourly wages for non-shift workers.
* Shift workers receive a monthly shift bonus of 10%, 15%, or 22.5% depending on the specific morning/evening/night shift rotation configured by the department head.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Human Resources ensures payroll configurations match statutory caps; Line Managers are responsible for structuring shift timetables within legal boundaries.
* **Approval Workflows:** Alterations to standard department shift models require explicit filing with Human Resources and final authorization by the Operations Director.
