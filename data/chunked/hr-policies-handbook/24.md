## 14. Recruitment & Hiring Policy
* **Definitions:**
* *Inclusive Sourcing:* Utilizing objective, neutral advertising text and diverse sourcing platforms to establish balanced talent pools.
* *Panel Evaluation:* A structured cross-functional interview layout designed to mitigate individual hiring bias.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Governs all talent sourcing activities, hiring pipelines, and interviewing tracks operated for the organization.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** The Senior HR Executive role designs job frameworks and screens resumes. Hiring Managers conduct specific panel reviews. Executive leadership provides headcount cost authorizations.
* **Approval Workflows:** Job opening initiation -> Budget sign-off by Finance -> Candidate selection panel consensus -> Executive offer validation by the CEO role.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* The company mandates equal opportunity hiring across all talent sourcing pipelines. All open job vacancies must utilize professional, neutral terminology to ensure fair and objective selection.
* Sourcing infrastructure relies on multi-layered assessment criteria, including technical screening, code evaluations where applicable, and standardized behavior-based panel interviews. ATS tools may assist with automated keyword screening while maintaining strict data privacy.
* No final candidate offer can be issued without completing a standard background and reference verification check to confirm educational and professional histories.
* Sourcing documentation, interview notes, and candidate portfolio databases are archived securely within the ATS according to localized candidate data privacy mandates for a maximum retention window of 1 year post-rejection, after which they are permanently deleted unless explicit candidate consent is achieved.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: How long is candidate data retained if they are not selected?* A: Up to 1 year within the ATS, under data privacy guidelines, before permanent deletion.
