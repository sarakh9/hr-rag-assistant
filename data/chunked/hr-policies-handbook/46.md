* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: What notice period is expected for a voluntary resignation?* A: A minimum of 1 month written notice in advance.
* *Q: How is severance pay calculated under this policy?* A: A minimum of 1 full month's last base salary for every year of continuous service rendered.
