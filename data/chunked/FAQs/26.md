### Q: How should I update my desk availability during short absences or lunch?
A: Temporary desk absences must be recorded transparently:
* *Short Breaks (1-2 Hours):* Update the dedicated "Office Availability" channel with estimated return times.
* *MS Teams Status Formatting:* Use **Available** for active work, **Be Right Back (BRB)** for temporary 10–15 minute breaks, **Away** (with a status message) for scheduled lunch breaks, and **Offline** (with a status message) for end-of-day logout or active approved leave.
