## 10. Whistleblower Policy & Anonymous Reporting
* **Definitions:**
* *Whistleblower Disclosure:* Reporting in good faith an observed breach of law, financial misconduct, or internal company policy.
* *Impartial Review Board:* The dedicated executive screening panel responsible for objective case triage.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Available to all internal employees, short-term contractors, external vendors, and business partners.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** The Whistleblower Review Panel (comprising Human Resources and Executive leadership) spearheads investigations. The Compliance Officer monitors anti-retaliation parameters.
* **Approval Workflows:** Disclosures are submitted to `ethics@company.com`. Preliminary evaluations occur within 5 business days to determine if a full scale inquiry launches.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* The Whistleblower Policy provides a safe mechanism for employees, contractors, and external partners to report suspected or verified violations of local laws, financial fraud, safety breaches, or code definitions.
* Disclosures can be routed confidentially to the designated Compliance Officer, submitted via email to `ethics@company.com`, or dropped into corporate physical suggestion boxes or secure online forms designed for anonymous entries.
* All submitted alerts are processed by the Whistleblower Review Panel, comprised of representatives from Human Resources and executive leadership. The panel handles preliminary assessments to confirm the validity and scope of the concern.
* **Investigation Timeline & Workflow:**
1. *Acknowledgment:* Receipt of a non-anonymous whistleblower alert is acknowledged within 3 to 5 business days.
2. *Preliminary Evaluation:* Initial data review is executed to determine if a formal investigation is warranted.
3. *Fact-Finding Inquiry:* An in-depth investigation is launched, utilizing interviews and secure document audits while maintaining strict data isolation.
