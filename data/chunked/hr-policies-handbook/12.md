* **Be Right Back (BRB):** Short temporary breaks lasting 10–15 minutes.
* **Away (with Status Message):** Scheduled lunch breaks or longer midday absences.
* **Offline (with Status Message):** End-of-day logout or active approved leave.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: What is the deadline to report an unplanned or emergency leave?* A: Between 9:30 AM and 10:00 AM, or prior to the daily morning scrum meeting, whichever is earlier.
* *Q: What Teams status should be selected for a 12-minute break?* A: Be Right Back (BRB).
