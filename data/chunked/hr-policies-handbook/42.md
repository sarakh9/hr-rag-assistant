## 27. Travel & Business Expense Policy
* **Definitions:**
* *Business Expense:* Necessary, documented costs incurred by an employee while executing official company tasks or traveling for corporate operations.
* *Per Diem Allowance:* A fixed daily fiscal supplement allocated to cover basic meal and incremental subsistence costs during corporate travel.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Covers all authorized employees traveling or spending personal funds for verified company business.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Employees collect valid invoices and receipts; Line managers review expense entries; Finance processes monthly reimbursements.
* **Approval Workflows:** Pre-travel authorization form -> Expense submission within 14 days -> Manager approval -> Finance payout.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* All business-related travel and high-value expense generation must receive explicit written authorization from the department head prior to booking or execution.
* The company reimburses valid, actual expenses for approved transport, lodging, and client hospitality, subject to pre-established budget limits and the delivery of official tax invoices.
* Employees must submit detailed expense reports paired with original receipts through the corporate expense system within 14 calendar days of completing travel. Uncertified or late receipts submitted past this window will be rejected.
* Utilizing business expense channels for personal gain or falsifying documentation constitutes corporate fraud and triggers evaluation under the disciplinary and legal framework.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: What is the deadline to submit travel receipts for reimbursement?* A: Within 14 calendar days of completing the travel or generating the expense.
* *Q: Can I book a business flight without manager approval and get reimbursed later?* A: No. All business travel requires written authorization form submission prior to booking.
