* Remote performance is monitored via objective deliverables and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) reviewed during regular evaluations. Managers provide structured feedback loops, and employees are encouraged to request clarification or operational support proactively.
* Continued non-compliance with WFH guidelines (such as unresponsiveness, unexcused meeting absences, unauthorized leave, or missed deadlines) triggers a progressive performance management sequence:
* *First Occurrence:* Verbal or written discussion and performance tracking.
* *Second Occurrence:* Temporary suspension of Work From Home privileges and transition to mandatory office check-ins.
* *Third Occurrence:* Review of remote work privileges, which may require a return to the physical office space, or further evaluation under the disciplinary framework.
* To support remote well-being and manage stress, employees are encouraged to structure their workflow efficiently, take short wellness breaks, and leverage corporate resources for mental health guidance or support assets.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: Can my family members use my company-provided laptop?* A: No. Complete device isolation is required; family members or third parties are restricted from accessing company systems.
* *Q: What happens on the second occurrence of violating WFH rules?* A: WFH privileges may be suspended temporarily, requiring physical check-ins at the office.
