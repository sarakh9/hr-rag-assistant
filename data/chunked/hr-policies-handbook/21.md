* Employees witnessing a violation of these guidelines should report it to their immediate manager, Senior HR, or executive leadership, or email `hr@company.com`.
* **Progressive Disciplinary Action Spectrum:** Verified violations result in corrective actions determined by Human Resources and management based on severity, frequency, and context. The framework focuses on remediation where appropriate:
* *Minor Offenses:* Verbal discussion or formal written warnings documented in the employee file.
* *Moderate Offenses:* Temporary suspension (with or without pay), performance monitoring logs, role reassignment, or temporary removal of operational privileges.
* *Severe Offenses:* Termination of employment or contractor engagement for cause, subject to complete legal and internal review.
* If a policy breach involves illegal activities (such as theft, corporate fraud, financial bribery, cyber property damage, or physical assault), the organization will pursue legal review to recover damages and cooperate with appropriate authorities.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: What data falls within the Confidential Information category?* A: Financial reports, forward strategies, salary structures, personnel assessments, client lists, source code, software architecture, internal memos, and meeting minutes.
* *Q: Are prescription medications allowed in the office?* A: Yes, provided they are legally authorized and used safely according to medical directions.
