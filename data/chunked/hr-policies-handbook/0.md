# HR Policies Handbook  
## 1. Document Management & Review Policy
* **Definitions:**
* *Policy Review Cycle:* The predetermined chronological schedule governing the evaluation of official company directives.
* *Review Triggers:* Specific external or internal occurrences that mandate immediate, unscheduled policy adjustments.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** This policy applies globally to all operational guidelines, handbooks, and regulatory compliance documents managed by the organization.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Policy management is overseen by Compliance Officers, Human Resources, Legal Counsel, or designated Policy Owners. Research, writing, and drafting are handled by the Senior HR Executive role, followed by vetting and formal validation from the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) role.
* **Approval Workflows:** Policy modification draft -> Vetted by Legal Compliance -> Authorized by Executive Leadership -> Communicated to the workforce.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* All organizational policies are reviewed annually or on an as-needed basis to maintain continuous alignment with regulatory requirements, industry best practices, and changing business objectives.
* Immediate, non-scheduled reviews can be triggered by new legislation, shifts in core business priorities, corporate restructuring, technological advancements, or compliance gaps identified during incident reviews.
* Updated policies feature a defined effective date, at which point they officially invalidate and supersede all prior iterations. Discrepancies or legacy disputes occurring prior to the effective date remain governed by the historical policy version in place at that time.
