## 23. Data Privacy & Employee Data Protection
* **Definitions:**
* *Personally Identifiable Information (PII):* Protected sensitive employee metrics (national IDs, banking links, home addresses, health logs).
* *RAG System Isolation:* Technical security parameters restricting automated search engines or AI assistants from exposing restricted employee data pools.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Binds all personnel managing corporate data, systems infrastructure, and automated information processing assets.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** The Data Protection Officer (DPO) role audits system infrastructure; IT maintains access profiles; all staff handle data securely.
* **Approval Workflows:** Access authorization requests for PII require dual written validation from the HR Director and the IT Security Lead.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* The company collects, processes, and maintains employee PII exclusively for legitimate operational, payroll, and statutory compliance reasons.
* Access to stored employee data files is limited strictly to authorized human resource specialists, legal compliance staff, and verified payroll administrators on a verified need-to-know basis.
* Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) models, internal AI assistants, and automated search indexes must be completely isolated from tables containing restricted employee PII, salary structures, or medical records to prevent unauthorized exposure during employee prompt processing.
* Employees have the right to request a formal inspection of their personal data files, review monitoring practices, and update missing or erroneous information by submitting a written request to the DPO role.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: Can the internal HR AI assistant access my salary details or performance logs?* A: No. RAG models and AI assistants are completely isolated from restricted employee PII and salary structures to guarantee absolute data privacy.
