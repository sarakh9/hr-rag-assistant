## 4. Gifts, Entertainment, & Cultural Customs
* **Definitions:**
* *Value Threshold:* The specified maximum monetary limit under which a business gift can be casually accepted without disclosure.
* *Cultural Exception:* Situations where refusing a token gift compromises standard regional diplomatic relations or professional etiquette.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Applies to all representatives of the company interacting with vendors, clients, public officials, or partners.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** Employees must proactively declare items. Human Resources and Compliance Officers review and allocate items.
* **Approval Workflows:** Gifts exceeding the standard company threshold require prior written authorization from the Compliance Officer or Human Resources before retention or processing.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* All business gifts, entertainment, or hospitality offers exceeding the company’s specified value threshold must be reported and formally documented.
* If declining a business gift is entirely impractical due to local cultural customs or etiquette, the employee must accept the item on behalf of the company and promptly report it to Human Resources for guidance.
* In scenarios where a received gift cannot be personally kept by an employee under policy thresholds, management will determine the appropriate disposal channel, which may include donating the item to charity, sharing it collectively with the team, or registering it as a permanent company asset.
* Accepting any exception to standard gift thresholds or engaging in entertainment patterns with active vendors requires prior written authorization from the Compliance Officer or Human Resources.
* **FAQs for RAG Retrieval:**
* *Q: What do I do if I cannot refuse a gift due to regional corporate customs?* A: Accept it politely on behalf of the company and immediately notify Human Resources for structural tracking.
