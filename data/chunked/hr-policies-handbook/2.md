## 2. Code of Business Conduct & Ethics
* **Definitions:**
* *Corporate Integrity:* Adherence to ethical values and professional transparency in all transactions.
* *Self-Declaration:* Formally signing an electronic or physical document testifying to total adherence to compliance mandates.
* **Eligibility Rules & Scope:** Every employee, contractor, business partner, vendor, and relevant stakeholder associated with the organization must comply with this Code.
* **Responsibility Matrix:** All corporate participants are responsible for their individual conduct. Human Resources and the Policy Compliance Committee (PCC) oversee compliance monitoring.
* **Approval Workflows:** Clarifications or updates regarding ethical codes are routed to Human Resources (`hr@company.com`), and escalated to the CEO (`ceo@company.com`) or the Policy Compliance Committee (PCC) for definitive validation.
* **Operational Procedures:**
* The organization is committed to professional growth founded upon transparency, responsibility, corporate integrity, compliance, and an inclusive work environment. Open communication of operational choices forms the core of stakeholder, client, and team relations.
* Every employee, contractor, business partner, and relevant stakeholder is expected to read, understand, and provide written or electronic self-declarations acknowledging their commitment to the Code of Conduct upon onboarding and following any major updates. Non-compliance with the acknowledgement mandate can prevent employment or business engagement continuation.
* Daily execution of responsibilities demands honesty in communication, protection of client and company data, timely reporting of conflicts of interest, and fair treatment across all business networks.
